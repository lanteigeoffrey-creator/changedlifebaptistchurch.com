Oasis — CLBC on campus.

What began as a prayer meeting on the University of Ghana campus in 2017 has grown into Oasis, CLBC's campus movement — now present at three universities and reaching senior high schools besides.

The Oasis story

From one prayer meeting to a movement

In 2017, Robert Koomson and Ernestina Ntim led a small prayer meeting at the University of Ghana, Legon, supported by the youth of Life Cathedral. Rev. Frederick Anim Oturoku took oversight that same year, and the ministry spread — to Ho in 2018, to KNUST in 2023, and to Cape Coast in 2024. In late 2025, the campus ministries were unified under one name: Oasis. On 18th January 2026, the maiden Oasis service was held at the University of Ghana's Alex Kwapong Hall Conference Room.

Legon
University of Ghana

Oasis UG

Re-branded in late 2025 to reach the university's 50,000+ students, Oasis UG holds Sunday service at 7:00am and a midweek fellowship at 7:00pm, together with sister ministry Oasis UPSA.

Alex Kwapong Hall Conference Room

Ho
University of Allied Health Sciences

Oasis UHAS

Planted in 2018 by Rev. Alfred Nyarko Mante and Mrs Veronica Salia, the Ho fellowship grew from three students to reach the Nursing Training College and Ho Technical University. Its annual "Kristonival" praise festival draws students from across the region.

Kumasi
KNUST

Oasis KNUST

Established in March 2023 with four pioneering members led by Ato Kwamina Donkor and Priscilla Bonney, the KNUST fellowship continues to grow every semester.

Oasis Youth Chapel

Worship for the 20–30s, on and off campus

Launched in November 2023 at Life Cathedral, Oasis Youth Chapel was built for young adults seeking a sanctuary that speaks their language — biblically grounded and culturally relevant. Expect "Digging Deeper" Bible discussions, a legendary post-service brunch, and the flagship "Vibing with Jesus" evangelism campaign.

Beyond the lecture hall

High schools reached through campus outreach

Oasis teams have run conferences and outreach visits at senior high schools including Akuse Methodist, Abuakwa State College, Diaspora Girls, Aburi Girls, Agotime, Winneba, Mfantsipim, Mamfe Girls and Osei Tutu — offering guidance on academics, entrepreneurship and spiritual growth, alongside the "10 Before 12" WhatsApp prayer platform.
