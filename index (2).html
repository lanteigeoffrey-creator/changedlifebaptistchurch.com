Our story

From a school porch in North Kaneshie to a nationwide church family.

In April 1976, a small group gathered under Rev. Solomon Owolabi's leadership on the porch of Ann's Preparatory School. Fifty years, seven pastors, and one name change later, Changed Life Baptist Church is a mother church — Life Cathedral — with thirteen branches and a growing campus movement, still standing on the same conviction that brought it into being.

  • Led by General Overseer Rev. Dr. Prince Sam Achin since 2000
  • Renamed from North Kaneshie Baptist Church to CLBC in 2012
  • Home of the End Time Bible College, founded 2007
