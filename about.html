Home / Our Story

Fifty years of changed lives, one church family.

Formerly North Kaneshie Baptist Church, CLBC was founded in 1976 by a Nigerian Baptist missionary with a small congregation and a big faith. This is the road from a school porch to a nationwide fellowship.

Vision

A Changed Life for Christ

Every branch, ministry and campus fellowship exists to see lives transformed by the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Mission

Growing Christlike disciples

We grow members into Christlike disciples, minister through worship and evangelism, and partner with like-minded believers — by the power of the Holy Spirit.

Roots

Fundamental Baptist heritage

Founded in North Kaneshie in 1976 with doctrinal roots as a Fundamental Baptist church, supported by Baptist International Missions Incorporated (USA).

Our history

1976 to today

1976

North Kaneshie Baptist Church is founded

Rev. Solomon Owolabi, a Nigerian Baptist missionary supported by Baptist International Missions Incorporated (USA), gathers a small congregation on the porch of Ann's Preparatory School. The first Sunday Morning Fellowship Service is held on 18th April 1976.

1977–81

Interim leadership and return

Rev. Owolabi leaves for further studies in the USA in 1977, placing the church under missionary Rev. Johnny Todd. He returns in 1978 and leads until 1981, when Ghana's political revolution forces his departure for Nigeria — leaving the Body of Deacons to shepherd the church.

1981

The first Bible School — and the first branch

NKBC becomes the lead contributor to the first Bible School run jointly by four Fundamental Baptist churches. In 1984, a young member, Stephen Brenya, plants the church's first branch at Donkoase, Ashanti Region — known today as Living Word Temple.

1982–98

A season of pastors and deacons

Rev. Christian Dogor serves 1982–1984. The deacons lead again until Rev. Josiah Adjei Mante is called as Head Pastor in 1985, guiding the church for thirteen years. In 1987 the church purchases land in North Kaneshie — the site that would later become Life Cathedral.

1999–2000

A one-year bridge to lasting leadership

Rev. Philip Lutterodt joins for a single, strategic year in 1999 and helps secure Rev. Prince Sam Achin, who takes over as Head Pastor in 2000 and remains General Overseer to date.

2007

End Time Bible College founded

Under Rev. Dr. Prince Sam Achin, the church launches its own ministerial training school at Life Cathedral. By 2025 the college counts 190 graduates.

2009

Life Cathedral construction begins

Construction starts on the Life Cathedral building, and the church's first three-year strategic plan (2010–2012) is drafted — its first formal roadmap.

2012

North Kaneshie Baptist Church becomes CLBC

The church is renamed Changed Life Baptist Church, uniting all existing and future branches under one recognisable identity and banner.

2015

Land title secured

The church completes registration of its land and secures full title — closing a chapter that began with the original 1987 purchase.

2017–23

Campus and youth ministry take root

A prayer meeting at the University of Ghana, Legon grows into an organised Campus Ministry. Oasis Youth Chapel launches in 2023 at Life Cathedral under Rev. Dr. Kwabena Appiah Gyekye and Dc. David France.

2026

Golden Jubilee — 50 years

CLBC celebrates fifty years of ministry. Oasis UG holds its first service on 18th January 2026 at the Alex Kwapong Hall Conference Room, University of Ghana — a new chapter for the next fifty years.

Leadership

Who leads Changed Life Baptist Church

Under the covering of our General Overseer, a national team of Pastors, Deacons and Trustees carries the vision across every branch and ministry.

General Overseer

Rev. Dr. Prince Sam Achin

Head Pastor and General Overseer since 2000. His leadership has been marked by expansion, ministry diversification and strategic planning across the CLBC family.

Trustees

Deacon Dr. Lawrence Oware-Gyekye

Chairperson of the Trustees Board — the legal custodians of church property and stewards of compliance and governance.

Body of Deacons

Deacons & Deaconesses

A national body of deacons and deaconesses who have shepherded the church through every season of transition, in Accra and across every branch.

Golden Jubilee Committee

Rev. Alfred Nyarko-Mante & Dc. Spencer Badu

Chairman and Co-Chairman of the Golden Jubilee Planning Committee, leading the church's 50th anniversary celebrations.
