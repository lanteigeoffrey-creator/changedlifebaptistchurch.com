A ministry for every season of life.

From children just learning to memorise scripture to pastors' wives carrying the weight of ministry alongside their husbands, CLBC has built a place for every member to grow and serve.

Since 1976

Ladies' Ministry

The oldest ministry in the church, born the same year as CLBC itself. Women in Prayer (WIP) meetings anchor half-yearly seasons of intercession for family, youth, church and nation, alongside the Men and Women's Conference and an annual National Camp. In recent years the ministry has added health awareness sessions on menopause and breast cancer screening to its long tradition of fellowship and outreach.

Since 1999

Men's Ministry

Founded during the pastorate of Rev. Philip Lutterodt to give men a united voice in the church. Today the fellowship runs health talks, financial education and evangelism drives, with active branches in Pokuase, Ashongman and Anyaa alongside the main congregation at Life Cathedral.

National ministry

Pastors' Wives Ministry

Established by Mrs Charlotte Achin, wife of the General Overseer, to give the women behind CLBC's clergy a place of their own — prayer retreats, capacity-building and mentorship for wives carrying the unique weight of ministry life. Guided today by a National Executive Team across every branch.

Youth Ministry

Grounding young people in Scripture and Baptist doctrine through Bible study, prayer meetings, leadership training, camps and outreach — a training ground that has raised many of CLBC's current leaders.

Since 2023

Oasis Youth Chapel

Worship built for the 20–30 generation at Life Cathedral: "Digging Deeper" Bible discussions, the flagship "Vibing with Jesus" evangelism campaign, and a legendary post-service brunch that keeps the fellowship going long after the final song.

Since 2009

Bethel Teens' Chapel

Founded with fourteen young pioneers under Pastor Paul Dzadey, Bethel's philosophy has always been "flexible but firm" — scripture memorisation, frank monthly "Social Discussions" on peer pressure and social issues, and a pulpit regularly handed to graduating Bible School students.

Children's Ministry

Nurturing the church's youngest members in the knowledge of Christ from their earliest years, working hand in hand with AWANA and the Sunday School tradition that has shaped CLBC since its founding.

Since 1987

AWANA

"Approved Workmen Are Not Ashamed" (2 Timothy 2:15) — a global children's discipleship programme built on Handbook Time, Counsel Time and Game Time. AWANA has become a primary pipeline into CLBC leadership, with many of today's leaders tracing their spiritual growth back to the club.

Lay Ministry

Non-ordained members who serve as a bridge between clergy and congregation — Bible readers and service moderators who keep worship running with order and excellence, grounded in the doctrine of the priesthood of all believers.

10th anniversary, 2026

CLBC Educational Trust Fund

What began as a scholarship fund has grown into a trust supporting any brilliant child whose family cannot meet the cost of education. In ten years: 34 beneficiaries, 23 scholars supported at university level, and 13 graduates now serving in fields from Medicine to Cyber Security — funded by over a quarter of a million Ghana Cedis in church and member giving.

"Indeed, grace is a river that refuses to stay within its banks." — on the growth of the CLBC Educational Trust Fund
