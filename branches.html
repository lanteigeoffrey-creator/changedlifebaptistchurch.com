Home / Branches

One church, thirteen branches, five regions.

From a single congregation in North Kaneshie, CLBC has grown into a family of churches planted through mission teams, evangelism campaigns and everyday faithfulness. Find the one nearest you.

Greater Accra Region
Life Cathedral North Kaneshie, Accra

The mother church — home of the General Overseer, the End Time Bible College and Oasis Youth Chapel.

Mother church
Deliverance Centre Ashongman, Greater Accra

Planted 2011 from a classroom mission to a permanent sanctuary, led by Rev. Richard Asante Sarpong.

Love Temple Pokuase, Greater Accra

Sparked in 2014 by a Youth on Missions team; now a congregation of 150 preparing to plant again.

Rev. Mark Ewudzie Memorial Temple Anyaa (Miracle Centre)

Joined the CLBC family in 2015, carrying forward the legacy of the late Rev. Mark DeGraft Ewudzie.

Grace Life Temple West Hills, Greater Accra

Inaugurated September 2017 on the word "Building unto Posterity"; now transitioning to a permanent home.

Ashanti Region
Living Word Temple Donkoasi, Ashanti Region

CLBC's very first branch, planted in 1984 — forty-two years of ministry in Donkoasi.

Founding branch
Salvation Temple Bekwai, Ashanti Region

Planted through an evangelism campaign in partnership with JoyfulWay Inc.

Christ Temple Ahenema Kokoben, Ashanti Region

A decade of ministry anchored in Lamentations 3:22-23.

Calvary Temple Meduma, Ashanti Region

A decade old and still growing a godly generation in Meduma.

Zoe Temple Oduom, Kumasi, Ashanti Region

Seven years young and already a testimony of transformed lives in Kumasi.

Eastern Region
Redemption Temple Asokore, Koforidua, Eastern Region

Planted February 2020 from just six people; today a congregation of over sixty building a permanent temple.

Western Region
Grace Temple Daboase, Western Region

"Hitherto hath the Lord helped us" — 1 Samuel 7:12, the branch's founding conviction.

Bible Temple Supomu Dunkwa, Western Region

Steadfast in spreading God's Word in the Supomu Dunkwa community.

Central Region
Glory Temple Cape Coast, Central Region

Birthed through a humble, God-fearing founder whose spiritual fire has swept across the nation.
